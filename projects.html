EEG quality control on a public dataset

Resting-state EEG from OpenNeuro (ds003775), cleaned and written up as a decision journal. The first quality check flagged 57 to 64 of 64 channels as bad in every subject. That was the check failing, not the data: raw DC offset made every channel look like an outlier. I discarded the pass and reran it on corrected data. The journal records every choice and seven limitations that argue against the result.

Beta bursts for brain-computer interfaces in patients

My master's thesis at INSERM Lyon (CRNL), supervised by Jérémie Mattout and James Bonaiuto. I tested whether a published beta-burst method for decoding imagined movement works in patients who cannot move: locked-in, tetraplegic and disorders of consciousness. It reproduced in healthy controls and did not transfer to patients. I am now extending the work on public datasets.

Sleep staging from a wrist sensor

Can sleep stages be read from the pulse signal of a wrist wearable (PPG)? Built on the public DREAMT dataset, each person scored separately. Feature groups are added one at a time, and each is kept only if it improves on the last.
