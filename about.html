Hi, I'm Divyanshi.

I enjoy working with biosignals to understand and solve problems, and to learn what works, what doesn't, and what seems to work but might not.

For my master's thesis at INSERM Lyon, I worked with EEG from patients who can't move. I tried to decode imagined hand movements from beta bursts. It worked in healthy people and not in the patients, and I'm still curious why.

At Dognosis I worked as a Research Associate. I led research paradigms, collected and analysed the data, and designed the quality checks for the experiments. A lot of that turned out to be finding checks that looked fine but had quietly stopped working.

Before all this I studied biology and data science at IISER Mohali. Now I'm extending the thesis work on public data, and learning sleep staging from a wrist pulse sensor.
